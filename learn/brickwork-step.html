2. The four kinds of gate

gatebricksCNOTs eachchanges bits?what it does
pair gate1–6, 12–154yesa particle hops between neighbours (kinetic strength 0.30)
SWAP7–11, 16–173only moves thembrings new neighbours together for the next pair gates; no physics
electric gate18–232no, phase onlyenergy of the electric field (the string) on one site
mass Rz— (one-qubit)0no, phase onlyenergy cost of a particle on a site

Per step at 12 qubits: 10 pair + 7 SWAP + 6 electric = 73 CNOTs (40 + 21 + 12), before the transpiler merges anything.

Only the pair gate moves particles. The electric and mass gates only add phases — but at the next step the pair gates mix terms, and those phases decide which hops add up and which cancel. Phase = energy: they steer the motion without moving anything themselves.

3. What changed from the earlier circuit

  • Pair-gate Rz values: ±0.15 → ±0.3 (kinetic 0.15 → 0.30).
  • Electric Rz values: ±0.005 → ±0.113 (field 22× stronger).
  • Mass signs: per qubit (− + − + …) → per site (− − + + …). See Mass term.
  • Hadron starting state: 2 flipped qubits in the middle → 4.
  • Number of steps: 20 → 11.
  • Bricks, order and qubit pairs: unchanged.

The worked examples on the pair gate and electric gate pages use the earlier angles (0.15 and 0.005). Same gates, only the angles differ.
