4. Zeroing out ZZ: done (update 23.10.2026)

  • Every two-qubit block in IBM's trainable circuit is exp(i(a XX + b YY + c ZZ)) plus one-qubit turns: 3 angles, written with 3 CNOTs.
  • Pair-gate (hop) and electric blocks start with c = 0. Keep c at 0 during training and each needs only 2 CNOTs. At 12 qubits that is 80 of 127 blocks; the 47 SWAP blocks keep all 3.
  • Now in our script: --zero-out-zz.
